Guest Info

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?+
Cocktail or Formal Attire.

🚫 Please avoid: jeans, shorts, t-shirts/casual shirts, and slippers.

See our full Attire Guide section above for color palette and style inspiration. ✨
Can I bring children?+
Please notify CJ or Sheilla directly if you plan to bring children. Our venue has a limited capacity and every seat must be accounted for in advance. 🌸
When is the RSVP deadline?+
Please confirm by October 1, 2026.

⚠️ Please strictly follow the number of reserved seats on your invitation. Our venue has a limited number of seats and we cannot accommodate guests beyond your allocated seats.
Is there parking available?+
Yes — parking is available on-site at both the ceremony and reception venues. 🚗
Can I take photos during the ceremony?+
The ceremony will be unplugged — please keep devices away so we can all be fully present. Our professional photographers will capture every moment beautifully.

📷 Photos are very welcome after the ceremony, during cocktail hour, and throughout the reception!
Where should I stay?+
Please refer to our Travel & Accommodations section on this page for our recommended hotels in Alabang, Muntinlupa — all within easy reach of both venues. Book early as December fills up fast! 🏨
How do I get from the ceremony to the reception?+
From Iglesia Ni Cristo, Paranaque to La Faite Country Garden, San Pedro, Laguna.

How do I get in touch?+
Feel free to reach out directly to CJ or Sheilla, or contact our wedding coordinator for any questions. We're happy to help! 💛
